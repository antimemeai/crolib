# Software lane: acquired references and static capability audit

Inspection date: 2026-10-02. This lane preserves the original sixteen catalogue records and extends them to **37 records: 33 acquired, two metadata-only, two access-blocked**. Acquired catalogue artifacts comprise sixteen PDFs, one original distribution HTML page, and sixteen restored source-document records. The additional archived EduG description page is acquired and provenance-recorded but is supporting evidence rather than a distinct bibliographic work. Root's package inventory remains separate: a literature record, a manual, an archive, a wrapper, and an independent estimator are different things.

The capability matrix covers **19 reference families**. [`capability-matrix.json`](capability-matrix.json) carries estimator, design, score scale, negative/boundary policy, uncertainty target, fixture provenance and the exact inspected paths. [`capability-matrix.md`](capability-matrix.md) is the compact comparison. Every row is static manual/source inspection. No acquired software was run, compiled, installed, imported, or adopted as a dependency. Package restore/hash validation is root's responsibility and evidence; it does not establish numerical conformance.

## Primary software literature and historical lineage

[Bell's 1985 original paper](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3102/10769986010001019) was acquired through the supplied CiteSeer URL, whose ordinary redirect reaches an archived PDF. The paper explains why calculating ANOVA sums of squares through full design matrices can be impractical for single-observation-per-cell GT data, distinguishes balanced EMS estimators from unbalanced alternatives, and develops SAS VARCOMP MIVQUE0. Its SAS/SPSS performance and feature statements describe 1985 implementations. They motivate estimator/algorithm comparisons without becoming claims about present products. The complete paper is local; the design-matrix argument, MIVQUE0 derivation, example and conclusions were read.

[FANTAB (Brown, 1969), ERIC ED143704](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED143704) is an important earlier *symbolic design compiler*: two to four factors, optional nesting, and random/finite/fixed factor statuses produce sources, SS/df/MS formulas, EMS and F-ratios. ERIC says the 93-page thesis has program and example appendices. Only primary metadata/abstract were available. It is not evidence that a recovered FANTAB engine has fitted any observations. The unavailable thesis and program appendix belong on the operator acquisition list.

Brennan's **GAPID, ACT Technical Bulletin 34, October 1979**, is verified in the primary [Brennan/Lockwood 1980 reference list](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/014662168000400209). It is the historical single-facet FORTRAN IV program. A related handbook catalogue describes 136 pages, but the manual/source have not been acquired or read. The contaminated GoogleBooks 1974 authorship result was excluded. Neither GAPID nor FANTAB should inflate the count of restored programs.

The official GENOVA-family manuals provide the strongest design-specific legacy examples. GENOVA and **jGENOVA** belong to the same algorithmic lineage; a Java translation adds available source, not an automatically independent numerical oracle. jGENOVA's manual restricts designs to two through six facets, at least two levels for each, balanced/orthogonal data and a complete set of interactions. It supports fixed/finite universes, mean-square or variance-component inputs, component SEs/covariances, D studies and cutpoint indices. Selected `Dcalc.java` paths show finite-universe/sample transformations and covariance propagation. `GENinp.txt` paired with `GENout.txt` and printed manual outputs are available fixtures. The source archive and distributed executable are retained separately; they are not assumed to be byte-for-byte the same build merely because both say version 1.0.

**urGENOVA** is explicitly different: Henderson Method I/analogous ANOVA for complete all-random designs, including unequal nesting, unequal cell replication and empty cells, but **G-study component estimation only**. Its manual explicitly disclaims a compelling universally-best estimator for unbalanced data. It describes efficient ANSI C algorithms, but the acquired legacy distribution supplies binaries, not that C source. Balanced negative-component comparisons require GENOVA's ALGORITHM option. **mGENOVA** supplies multivariate components and D studies for an enumerated group of designs, profile variation, conditional SEM and regressed/composite scores. Its `RCG Data`, control cards and manual appendices form a rich fixture bank. They do not establish an arbitrary-design multivariate engine or a general PSD repair guarantee.

## EduG recovery and the finite-universe distinction

The original **EduG 6.1 user guide dated 2 March 2010** was recovered from an ordinary public Wayback copy of its original [IRDP-hosted PDF](https://www.irdp.ch/data/secure/1968/document/EduGUserGuide.pdf). Its direct current/migrated paths returned missing or non-PDF pages. The original distribution HTML was also recovered. This is primary archived material, not a manual aggregator's reconstruction.

The guide limits raw-data ANOVA to complete balanced crossed/nested designs with at most eight facets; imported approximate SS from other software are a separate workflow. It identifies arbitrary differentiation and instrumentation faces, fixed/finite-random/infinite facets, and confounding when a differentiation facet is nested inside an instrumentation facet. Its **Coef_G** applies Whimbey factors `(N−1)/N`; according to the universe/object setting it can be traditional rho-squared, omega-squared or an intermediate coefficient. The numerator and denominator in Appendices A/B must therefore be matched before comparing its printed “G” with another engine. Finite-universe corrections and measurement-object symmetry are substantial design algebra, not extra labels on one p×i×r formula. The guide prints negative component estimates and uses zero replacements downstream.

The original distribution page contains an essential warning: **EduG v5 incorrectly calculated some interactions involving fixed facets; the error was corrected in v6**. The accompanying book's worked examples used v5, and the page continued offering that version for those examples. Book agreement alone is consequently a version-dependent oracle for fixed-facet cases. The recovered page names `InstallerEduG61e.zip` (1.9 MB) but uses a Flash download button; installer/source was not recovered. Two public archive CDX wildcard lookups found no ZIP captures. The current IRDP [Windows note](https://www.irdp.ch/data/documents/Avertissement_windows-seven-vista_en.pdf) was acquired and read; it concerns historical APLgrid.dll registration on XP/Vista/7 and was not executed as current setup guidance.

The newly acquired [SAGT primary article (2016)](https://www.riped-online.com/articles/sagt-new-software-for-generalizability-analysis.pdf) identifies another important GUI family: four languages, up to sixteen facets, optimization/plots and import from GT/EduG. Software-description pages were read in Spanish alongside the English abstract. Its installer/manual were advertised at MenPas. The current MenPas page redirects to a university-hosted legacy ASP.NET site; no source or binary was acquired. Capabilities remain article-advertised, not source-verified. Ysewijn's older GT/GT-E lineage is documented in the EduG and SAGT original materials but a separate original 1996 manual/distribution remains unavailable.

## Modern engines: inspected behavior and limits

**GeneralizIT** supplies a useful owned-looking analogous-ANOVA reference, with T values, a linear coefficient system, eight enumerated one/two-facet crossed/nested design patterns, and source fixtures for fixed, missing and unbalanced examples. Its 2024 preprint is narrower than the current restored source and the 2025 final abstract; the final SoftwareX article remains blocked. The source keeps signed ANOVA values but clips negative values when deriving coefficients and mean intervals; supplied component dictionaries take a stricter nonnegative path. `calculate_confidence_intervals()` is a **plug-in normal interval for facet-level means**, not an estimation CI for variance components, G, Phi or a projected design. Fixed-facet/Whimbey handling is present, but a general user-specified finite-universe API was not established. Brennan's synthetic datasets 1–8, including unbalanced 4 and missing 1, contain explicit T/component/coefficient targets and are more useful than generic workflow examples.

**facet** is a broad framework combining aov, lme4 and brms. Selected code confirms ANOVA component clipping at zero, Satterthwaite-derived approximate df/SE followed by *normal Wald* bounds clipped below at zero, and missing SE/bounds when the clipped component is zero. This is not an exact chi-square inversion or an established boundary-coverage guarantee. lme4 profile/parametric bootstrap dispatch is explicit; Bayesian/multivariate/posterior support is advertised and partially reflected in source. The scope of arbitrary-design EMS and aov covariance construction was not exhaustively proved: the inspected `(MS−residualMS)/n_eff` path especially needs independent complete three-way/design-specific EMS checks before serving as an oracle. Fixtures carefully distinguish globally unique raters/items from reused labels and residual confounding. Some tests verify shape/dispatch, while D-study tests verify meaningful algebraic rescaling, aggregation and universe/error partitioning; do not equate their evidential strength.

**G_String_MV/M** is a modern Java GUI and downstream decision/synthesis layer whose component fitting invokes bundled **urGENOVA executables** through `ProcessBuilder`. It is not a second independent component estimator. Java component parsing clips negatives and differentiates differentiation, stratification and generalization roles before scaling. `Cases/` contains MCQ/OSCE/REP data, controls and printed results. README/suffix “MV” is not enough to establish an independently implemented general multivariate covariance engine. The wrapper's GPL license does not settle the source or license of its bundled urGENOVA artifacts.

**gtheory** calls lme4 REML; its stratified multivariate implementation fits strata separately, calculates covariance of observed object means between strata, and treats relative/absolute error covariance matrices as diagonal. That is an explicit statistical scope, not general multivariate fitting of each shared facet's covariance matrix. Its unbalanced D-study path retains object-specific divisors and presents an overall median-divisor summary. Examples point to Brennan's pp.116 and 270–272. The archived package author is Christopher T. Moore; [Huebner/Lucht's acquired 2019 tutorial](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1222666.pdf) explains its use and is not package authorship. **gtheoryr** is a much smaller closed-form balanced p×i/i:p implementation, retaining signed components, with two CSV examples. Entire numerical source was read. It has no interval machinery; missing/nonfinite data, singleton df, denominator degeneracy and proposed noninteger/nonfinite counts warrant independent specification before copying any behavior.

**csemGT** is explicitly restricted to complete numeric p×i conditional SEM, on the mean-per-item score scale. A cutpoint must use that scale. Negative component/conditional-error clipping is optional and defaults off; signed conditional variances can yield `NaN` SEM. The source accepts percentile/basic/BCa interval names, but v1.0 always uses **normal Wald intervals**, recording requested/effective method metadata. Algebraic Lord/binomial and D-study identities are valuable independent fixtures; legacy R parity is shared-lineage regression. A Stata-parity test exists but the required `gtcsem_reference_results.dta` is **absent from the pinned source**, so it skips; only input/cutpoint RDS are installed.

The new [Gempp analytical-SE working paper](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/6qg9r_v1/) was acquired and targeted sections read. It derives exact sampling variances conditional on observed item difficulty under Gaussian residuals and treats estimated item variance as fixed plug-in. This differs from universe-of-items resampling uncertainty. The author explicitly defers finite-sample/non-Gaussian interval coverage to v2. The linked project `8xvyb` currently exposes only its PDF, despite the manuscript's code/supplement assertion. The separate June2026 csemGT software article has verified DOI `10.1177/01466216261461571` but its PMC fulltext returned 403; source/manual/preprint do not substitute for reading that article.

**gt4ireval** is a clean domain-specific independent p×q ANOVA reference: systems are measurement objects and queries a facet; coefficients, query-count planning and F-based intervals are explicit. Its synthetic fixture asserts numerical targets from Table6.2 p182. The [original Urbano/Marrero/Martín 2013 paper](https://julian-urbano.info/files/publications/055-measurement-test-collection-reliability.pdf) and current manual were acquired. Mappings from GT coefficients to Kendall correlation/sensitivity are domain-specific additions.

**intraclass** provides a substantial modern comparison for ICC estimands, multiple mixed/SEM/Bayesian engines, identifiability guards, boundary-aware covariance Monte Carlo, bootstrap and coherent D-study reprojection across counts. Selected source rejects fixed-rater agreement projections and omits incomplete multilevel cluster projections whose ragged divisor is unresolved. Its Monte Carlo sampler clamps covariance eigenvalues at zero, including some genuine rank deficiencies by documented design; the estimand guards must be independently audited. Positivity after transformation is not an interval-coverage proof. A balanced base-R ANOVA oracle and cached Bayesian/published fixtures are valuable, but the whole 562-file development tree was not reviewed.

**bayesian.gtheory** propagates posterior draws through design divisors before computing quantiles and threshold probabilities. Its all-random crossed-two-facet `G_coef` includes item/occasion and interaction main-error terms and therefore targets **absolute Phi despite the G label**. The README assertion that frequentist intervals are impossible conflicts with the substantial interval literature; do not reproduce it. **dtheory** is another helper over gtheory, random p×t only, whose coefficient is also absolute agreement and whose analytical interval documentation assumes normal effects. Neither adds an independent component-estimation oracle.

The **lavaan author script** has explicit one/two/three crossed-facet branches, equal loadings and ULS/WLSMV theta/delta alternatives; ordinal latent scales must not be compared blindly with raw-score ANOVA variances. The **mixed-format Stan** supplement combines Gaussian free-response and Bernoulli-logit MCQ likelihoods with joint person covariance and format-specific components; empirical prior constants and response scales are study-specific. The acquired `ddp` and `noninfo` Stan files are byte-identical and both contain those empirical prior constants: their filenames do not provide two distinct sensitivity conditions. The **multilevel IRR Stan** bundle includes cluster, subject, rater and cluster×rater effects and separate agreement/consistency, single/average generated ICCs. These are acquired article-specific reference sources, not arbitrary-design libraries. **emreliability** is an adjacent CTT/IRT reliability/CSEM reference associated with Lee/Harris, and **Mushquash/O’Connor's seven-language-file bundle** distinguishes G1 estimation from supplied-component G2 projection; inspected G2.R clips supplied negatives. Neither should inflate independent multifacet fitting counts.

## Reuse boundaries, evidence and outstanding acquisition

Six package `license_note` fields were corrected from original files: gtheory GPL-3; gtheoryr MIT+LICENSE; bayesian.gtheory and dtheory MIT+LICENSE/full grant; intraclass MIT+LICENSE/full grant; gt4ireval MIT+LICENSE. facet/G_String GPL and GeneralizIT MIT originals were inspected. Public access is not a reuse license for author scripts, legacy binaries or OSF files; study-specific SEM/Stan bundles contain no inspected general source grant. The handwritten machinery and statistical specification for crolib remain owned work; no dependency or source transplant was selected.

The next research/design step is to select a **small independent fixture bank with declared estimands**: exact balanced two/three-way EMS cases; crossed and nested label invariance; finite/fixed universe endpoints; raw-versus-clipped components; jGENOVA/mGENOVA worked outputs with version/options; mean/sum score scaling; Lord CSEM identity; multivariate covariance PSD/aliasing; and propagated D-study intervals with explicit uncertainty targets. Wrapper agreement, copied datasets and shared backend agreement count as the same lineage of evidence. A source test that skips an absent fixture is not an acquired oracle.

Still needed: original FANTAB/GAPID source-bearing manuals; EduG6.1 installer/source and careful EduG5 fixed-facet errata reconciliation; SAGT/GT-E original manuals/distributions; GeneralizIT final2025 paper; csemGT2026 software article; Jiang/Skorupski original Bayesian-MGT supplement; original APA met0000606 and MDPI math13061001 supplements where other lanes have not recovered them. These are acquisition gaps, not reasons to invent support or bypass access controls. The search log records attempted primary URLs, archive lookups, failed downloads and actual read depths.
