## Design lessons that an owned engine should preserve

**Task replication and rater replication are different purchases.** Shavelson, Baxter and Gao’s performance-assessment sampling work distinguishes tasks, occasions, raters and methods, with task sampling particularly consequential in its mathematics/science examples. Its CRESST report is acquired. Language assessment supplies complementary GENOVA/FACETS work and a synthesis rather than a universal task/rater rule. GT estimates variance contributions for a specified design; many-facet Rasch analysis also addresses individual task difficulty/rater severity. These tools should not be presented as equivalent fits. [Shavelson et al. 1993](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1745-3984.1993.tb00424.x), [Bachman et al. 1995](https://doi.org/10.1177/026553229501200206), [In’nami–Koizumi 2016](https://doi.org/10.1177/0265532215587390).

**The target aggregation determines the coefficient.** Pharmacy provides compact worked examples: course grades use persons crossed with items nested in examinations; quizzes and a final examination form distinct correlated occasions in a multivariate, unbalanced composite design. The OSCE example has stations nested in weeks and acknowledges station/rater confounding. A library should retain facet nesting, component covariance, score weights, and the operational score definition, rather than translate every example into a fully crossed table. The primer also distinguishes generalization evidence from other parts of a validity argument. [Pharmacy primer](https://doi.org/10.24926/iip.v12i1.2131), [course grades](https://doi.org/10.24926/iip.v12i1.2925), [OSCE](https://doi.org/10.24926/iip.v12i1.2110), [quiz/exam composite](https://doi.org/10.24926/iip.v12i1.2235).

**Natural operational data often confound the facets of interest.** The mini-CEX laboratory study deliberately crosses21 residents, three encounters and three assessors to separate effects that ordinary clinical sampling conflates. Its D-study projects different operational assessor/encounter arrangements. Lin’s 2015 primary Monte Carlo study compares rating-slot transformations with blocks retaining actual raters in sparse language-assessment data. Neither rearrangement should silently change the named sampling universe. Teacher observation adds a hidden temporal facet: time of year can be confounded with lesson and can also represent systematic improvement, not exchangeable noise. [Mini-CEX](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10459-011-9343-y), [Lin 2015](https://michiganassessment.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/20.02.pdf.Res_.ApracticalGuidetoInvestigatingScoreReliabilityunderaGeneralizabilityTheoryFramework.pdf), [teacher observations over time](https://doi.org/10.1177/0013164419826237).

**Substantive signal depends on the question.** Lakes–Hoyt’s caregiver/child contextual study and clinical-child tutorial use observer/rater/context variance to investigate behavior, not simply maximize a single coefficient. Mindfulness and self-compassion studies interpret person-by-occasion variance as state sensitivity, while stable-trait inference can put it into error. A component does not become inherently unwanted because an ANOVA labels it an interaction. Likewise, low reliability after range restriction need not indicate a changed instrument: the person variance can have changed. [Lakes–Hoyt 2008](https://doi.org/10.1002/icd.551), [Lakes–Hoyt 2009](https://doi.org/10.1080/15374410802575461), [restricted sample variance](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0030912), [mindfulness 2017](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12671-017-0679-0), [self-compassion 2021](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12671-020-01522-3).

**Workplace applications need actual assessor identity and aggregation semantics.** Putka–Hoffman’s detailed decomposition and Jackson et al.’s Bayesian follow-on challenge confounded dimension/exercise interpretations. The 2022 comparison with latent-variable models shows that GT can inform structural-validity questions as well as reliability. These results are design-specific evidence, not proof that dimensions never matter. A further mathematical caution: the Jackson 2016 manuscript describes REML boundary estimates as negative variances subsequently fenced at zero. That language must not be copied into engine documentation as a general description of constrained REML. A boundary estimate, clipped moment estimate, and failed estimation are different states. [Putka–Hoffman 2013](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0030887), [Jackson et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1037/apl0000102), [Jackson et al. 2022 author manuscript](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/document/97064).

**Reliability is conditional on the physiology, preprocessing and sampling unit.** Rocha et al. 2026 spans ERP/EEG, electrodermal, electromyographic and cardiac measurements. Artifact rejection creates unequal trial counts; trial means, equal-length splits and nonlinear waveform summaries are not interchangeable. Repeated recording sessions and subtraction/residualized contrasts introduce different covariance requirements. Rast–Clayson 2026 extends the framework with mixed-effects location-scale models: residual variance, and consequently reliability, can vary with persons and conditions. Our engineering implication is to expose both population coefficients and their conditioning/model assumptions. Merely adding a participant-specific denominator does not establish a validated individualized measurement model. [Rocha et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijpsycho.2026.113321), [Rast–Clayson 2026](https://doi.org/10.1111/bmsp.70026), [difference-score methods](https://doi.org/10.1111/psyp.13762).

**A high traveling-subject coefficient can describe the wrong deployment design.** Gee et al. 2015 scans each traveling subject twice at each of eight sites. Cannon et al.’s 2018 clarification supplies the coefficient for a subject scanned once at one random site, which better matches many multisite cohorts. Cao et al.’s connectomic study similarly compares the crossed reliability study with that operational D-study. ICED models run/session/day/site error through SEM and explicitly addresses what the design can identify. These are unusually useful examples for preventing a library from returning an impressive coefficient for an unintended averaging design. [Gee et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.22791), [Cannon et al. 2018 clarification](https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.23875), [connectomic metrics](https://doi.org/10.1093/cercor/bhy032), [ICED 2018](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.35718), [Noble et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1093/cercor/bhx230).

**Information retrieval is an established bridge to machine evaluation.** Bodoff treats systems as measurement objects and queries/assessors as facets. Urbano et al. 2013 links GT coefficients to familiar ranking-stability measures and gives interval-based query requirements; Urbano 2016 provides simulation with known true system scores for evaluating bias and robustness. In the inspected gt4ireval reference, `drop` removes low-performing systems, so it changes the system population and potentially the signal variance. Its mappings from GT to Kendall/AP correlations should retain the calibration context instead of become universal mathematical identities. [Bodoff 2008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ipm.2007.11.006), [Urbano et al. 2013](https://doi.org/10.1145/2484028.2484038), [Urbano 2016](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10791-015-9274-y), [gt4ireval](https://github.com/julian-urbano/gt4ireval).

## AI evidence: relevant, recent, and provisional

Six works supply a contemporary application map. Five are currently catalogued as preprints: Srinivasan’s agent DDR, Żatuchin’s brand decomposition, Anand’s judge-sizing study, Liu’s annotation decomposition, and Song–Lee–Jiao’s AP Chinese writing autoscoring study. Wang et al.’s 2025 writing-rater paper is a distinct published article using GT and many-facet Rasch modeling. It is not the published version of the AP Chinese preprint. The existence of these papers establishes an active application direction; their numerical claims are not independent validation of a proposed Crolib engine. [DDR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11323), [brand answers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13304), [LLM judges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27787), [Liu annotation preprint](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6432598), [AP Chinese preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19980), [Wang et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2025.100481).

Three specific cautions follow from the acquired primary papers and inspected author documentation:

- DDR compares Gaussian ANOVA/REML variance components with binomial-GLMM posterior standard deviations on a logit scale, then squares the latter for comparability. Squaring a standard deviation does **not** move latent-logit variance to the observed binary scale. This is our mathematical assessment of the stated procedure, not an empirical reanalysis. Pilot-based omission of higher interactions and flooring negative moment estimates further limit its value as a numerical oracle. Its step observations may also require dependence and nesting checks.
- The judge-sizing paper’s system-by-judge component gives a model-specific one-judge reliability ceiling: adding items cannot average away a term that carries no item-count divisor. Pairwise scoring changes the outcome/protocol and introduces presentation-order questions. Its PDF says the crossed judgment data, calculator and audit code are author-on-request. The arXiv abstract and acquired PDF give different observation totals; we do not promote either total into a verified result.
- The brand paper’s public Zenodo links describe aggregate data. The response-level iteration table used for variance estimation is request-only. Gtheory4LLM’s eight named outcome sets come from **three** panels; alternative codings are not eight independent datasets. Its author README records omitted-item parser defaults to NORMAL, omitted raw texts/reference labels/API metadata, Gaussian working-score interpretations for mental-health codings, and limits preventing full-panel dense discrete fits. These constraints belong in any reproduction plan. [Gtheory4LLM source documentation](https://github.com/Veronica0206/Gtheory4LLM), [public annotation deposit](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/K9CAJ).

Reliable repeated scoring alone does not prove correctness, construct validity, fairness, deployment safety, or causal transportability. GT coefficients must be tied to the specified item/prompt/model/judge/language universe; they cannot establish robustness to arbitrary distribution shifts.

A useful future engine-validation sequence is: owned balanced fixtures against published tables; nested and sparse operational-design cases; multivariate composites/contrasts with covariance propagation; known-generating-parameter PsyRAT tutorials; then real ICED/EEG/TREC data. Matching a Bayesian software output also requires matching priors, parameterization, likelihood, seed, and posterior-summary definition. A CSV’s availability alone does not establish that its native measurement scale or complete fitting design is supported.

## Remaining coverage and acquisition work

The published application literature is much larger than these 49 works. Underrepresented areas include rehabilitation and other allied health, speech/hearing and motor measures, marketing/customer measures, sports and judging, organizational interviews/situational-judgment tests beyond assessment centers, ecological momentary assessment, and non-English methodological traditions. Educational performance and teacher observation contain many additional operational technical reports. These are expansion leads rather than an assertion that the catalog is exhaustive.

To close the most important reading gaps, obtain lawful institutional or author copies of the18 blocked originals, especially the two practical guides and the original ERP methods. The AI judge and brand replication inputs require author contact; sending messages is outside this lane’s authorization. Licence inspection is still needed before redistributing any acquired research data or copying reference implementation code into production. Raw fulltexts, bibliographic response bodies, and source archives are retained locally; tracked catalogue/notes/restoration metadata remain compact.
