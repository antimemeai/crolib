# Bibliographic identity and evidence-contract audit

Audit date: 2026-10-02. This is a semantic audit, separate from root's byte-integrity and restoration checks. No downloaded source was executed and no dependency was adopted.

## Scope and limits

I compared all eight lane catalogues by DOI/title/year/author identity, inspected every multi-alias group in the then-current root catalogue, checked software-documentation year statements, and reread selected acquired PDF title pages and substantive fulltexts plus blocked/metadata records. The lane input at first inspection contained 524 records; the root catalogue contained 259 consolidated records and 11 multi-alias groups. The coverage lane had recently expanded to 233 records, so that root output was a previous generation, not a complete representation of the latest lane inputs. Root owns regeneration. This is a targeted identity audit, not a line-by-line validation of every manuscript or every automatically discovered coverage citation.

Primary semantic checks included the Shavelson/CRESST and Bachman/ERIC precursors; Cannon, Briggs/Alzen and Noble PMC records; Baldwin blocked publisher metadata; the distinct AI writing papers; Brennan's book/edition records; Self–Liang, Jiang and Brennan cross-lane aliases; software manuals/source documentation; and the newly acquired Urbano replication source. Publisher-deposited Crossref records were queried directly for the two precursor-linked final articles, Baldwin, and Brennan's CTT comparison. NIH article HTML and acquired PDF title pages were examined locally.

## Corrections applied to applications

1. **A report is not evidence of acquisition of its related final DOI article.** The previously acquired Shavelson1993 record cited the September1993 journal DOI but pointed to CSE Technical Report361, March1993. The report lists **Shavelson, Gao, Baxter**, while the journal metadata lists **Shavelson, Baxter, Gao**. These are now separate records: `applications-shavelson-report1993` is acquired, DOI null, report-specific title/year/authors; `applications-shavelson1993` retains journal DOI `10.1111/j.1745-3984.1993.tb00424.x`, is metadata-only, and has no acquired local path. Evidence notes explicitly distinguish the documents. Neither the title-year union nor the DOI union should merge them. [Primary report](https://cresst.org/wp-content/uploads/TECH361.pdf), [journal identity](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1745-3984.1993.tb00424.x).

2. **A1993 conference paper is not an acquired1995 journal article.** ERIC ED368154 identifies a27-page paper presented at the15th Language Testing Research Colloquium, Cambridge, 2–4August1993. The final DOI record is the July1995 journal article. `applications-bachman-conference1993` now identifies the acquired conference document, DOI null, explicit version title; `applications-bachman1995` retains DOI `10.1177/026553229501200206` and is metadata-only. The spelling Judgments/Judgements and stable downloaded basename do not establish publisher-version identity. [ERIC identity](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED368154), [journal identity](https://doi.org/10.1177/026553229501200206).

3. **Markup is presentation metadata, not title text.** Removed `<scp>` tags from the Baldwin2015 title while preserving ERN and Pe. Crossref deposits those tags in the title. DOI, authors and year were otherwise verified. Original article remains blocked; no fulltext reading is claimed. [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/psyp.12401).

4. **Author abbreviations and collective authors clarified.** Expanded Briggs/Alzen to Derek C. Briggs and Jessica L. Alzen from primary PMC front matter. Cannon's clarification authors are Tyrone D. Cannon, Hengyi Cao, Daniel H. Mathalon, Dylan G. Gee, **on behalf of the NAPLS consortium**; the collective attribution now matches the primary citation metadata instead of an unqualified consortium shorthand. [Teacher article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7328239/), [Cannon clarification](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5718980/).

The applications catalogue now has **51 document/version records representing49 original work families:31 acquired,18 access_blocked,2 metadata_only**. There are still31 acquired fulltext artifacts; no new paper PDF was downloaded for this correction. Distinct precursor records increase bibliographic precision rather than suggesting two additional independent empirical studies. Authored `identity-corrections.py` applies these corrections idempotently to retained working metadata before `finalize.py`; the report and search log have been updated.

## Evidence contracts that must remain explicit

- Brennan2001book is metadata-only/access-blocked across lanes: publisher preface/table-of-contents reading is not acquisition of the book. Brennan1983 and1992Elements editions remain separate. Shavelson/Webb1991Primer remains separate from the2006encyclopedia chapter DOI, which a misleading third-party listing associates with the book.
- An accepted author manuscript can legitimately support acquisition of the same DOI work while still requiring a version note. Urbano2016's author PDF has an accepted2015manuscript header but its journal issue citation is2016; neither the2015repository slug nor acceptance date should overwrite publication year.
- Software documentation/source records are documents, not peer-reviewed articles. Their snapshot2026 year often means acquisition/inspection, not original software publication. gtheory's2016year is explicitly supported by DESCRIPTION dates; the gt4ireval2.0manual2017 and current source snapshot2026 are separate document identities.
- The Jiang2024article and its mixed-format Stan supplement are separate objects; similarly, tenHove multilevel article and mathematical supplement are independently acquired. Supplement availability does not establish acquisition of another article.
- Conference/report versions and chapters inside complete volumes require specific evidence notes. Decisions already identifies the2016optimization chapter's printed pages173–185, despite preserving its entire proceedings PDF. This is appropriate; full-volume acquisition is not full-volume reading.
- The2025DanSong/Won-ChanLee/HongJiao arXiv writing paper and2025YuehanWang etalpublished writing article have different authors/titles/DOIs. They must not be merged through topical similarity. The published paper remains blocked.
- AI preprints are acquired manuscripts, not validated reference methods. SSRNJinLiu is abstract/source-documentation evidence only. Judge/brand request-only inputs are still unavailable; no article acquisition implies complete replication data.
- Stable internal slugs sometimes retain discovery years2017 for issue-year2018/2019neuroimaging papers. Their catalogue year and explicit version notes take precedence over filename inference.
